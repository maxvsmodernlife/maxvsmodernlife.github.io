Privacy Policy – Leo Shorts Uploader

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What this app is

Leo Shorts Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, Instagram account and TikTok account for the series "Leo Lost in Time". It is not offered to the public. See also the Terms of Service and the home page.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy applies to data processed by Google.

Instagram and TikTok

For Instagram the app uses the official Instagram API (the Meta Platform Terms and the Instagram Privacy Policy apply). For TikTok it uses the official TikTok Content Posting API and Login Kit with the permissions user.info.basic and video.upload (the TikTok Privacy Policy applies). On TikTok videos are only sent to the owner's inbox as drafts.

What data the app accesses

  • Access and refresh tokens for the owner's own accounts (YouTube youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl, Instagram content publishing and comments, TikTok user.info.basic and video.upload).
  • The name and handle of the owner's own channel/account, to make sure uploads go to the right account.
  • The video files and their title, description, tags and first comment, which the owner created.

The app does not read, collect or store any other personal data, viewing data, comments or data of other people.

How data is stored and shared

Tokens are stored only in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer. No data is sent anywhere except to Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram and TikTok for the upload itself. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. No analytics, no tracking, no cookies on this website.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Instagram under Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting the Keychain entries "leo-shorts.youtube", "leo-shorts.instagram" and "leo-shorts.tiktok" removes the stored tokens from the computer.
