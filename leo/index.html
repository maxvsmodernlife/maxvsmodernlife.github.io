Leo Shorts Uploader

Home of the private upload tool for the short-video series Leo Lost in Time.

What it does

Leo Shorts Uploader is a small tool that runs only on its owner's own computer. It uploads short videos the owner created to the owner's own accounts:

  • YouTube channel Leo Lost in Time – uploaded as private videos, optionally scheduled with a publish time (YouTube Data API v3);
  • Instagram account – Reels via the official Instagram API;
  • TikTok account – sent to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft (Content Posting API, Upload); the owner finishes every post by hand in the TikTok app.

It also posts the owner's own first comment under the owner's own videos on YouTube and Instagram. The tool is not offered to anyone else and does not access other people's accounts or data.
