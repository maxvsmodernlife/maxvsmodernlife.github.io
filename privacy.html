Privacy Policy – Max Shorts Uploader

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What this app is

Max Shorts Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, Instagram account and TikTok account. It is not offered to the public. See also the Terms of Service.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy applies to data processed by Google.

Instagram and TikTok

For Instagram the app uses the official Instagram API (Meta Platform Terms apply). For TikTok it uses the official TikTok Content Posting API and Login Kit with the permissions user.info.basic and video.upload (TikTok Privacy Policy applies). On TikTok videos are only sent to the owner's inbox as drafts.

What data the app accesses

  • OAuth access/refresh tokens for the owner's own accounts (YouTube youtube.upload, Instagram content publishing, TikTok video.upload), used only to upload videos.
  • The video files and their title, description and tags, which the owner created.

The app does not read, collect or store any other personal data, viewing data, comments or data of other people.

How data is stored and shared

The token is stored only in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer. No data is sent anywhere except to Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram and TikTok for the upload itself. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. No analytics or tracking.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Deleting the Keychain entry "max-shorts.youtube" removes the stored token from the computer (likewise "max-shorts.instagram" and "max-shorts.tiktok"). Instagram access can be removed in the Instagram settings under Apps and websites, TikTok access in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Apps and services.
