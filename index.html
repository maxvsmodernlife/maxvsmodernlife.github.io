Max Shorts Uploader

Max Shorts Uploader is a private tool used by a single creator to upload their own animated short videos (“MaxVSModernLife”) to their own YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API.

It is not offered to other users. It runs locally on the creator's own computer, uploads only videos the creator made, and does not read, modify or delete any other content.
